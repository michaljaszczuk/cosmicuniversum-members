Privacy note: birth chart report

This note is about the paid birth chart report only.

What we collect

  • Your name, as you want it printed.
  • Your email address.
  • Your birth date, birth time and birth town.
  • Where your birth time comes from and how sure you are of it.
  • Your two ticks on the order form, with the time you gave them.

Why

We use this data only to make your report and send it to you. We do not use it for anything else. We do not sell it.

Waitlists

If you cannot order yet (no birth time, or a birth town too far north or south), you can leave your email. For the town list we also keep your town. We use this only to tell you when the report works for you. Sender.net keeps the list. You can unsubscribe at any time, and then we delete it.

Who helps us

  • Cloudflare runs our website and stores your order and your report.
  • Gumroad takes your payment. Gumroad has its own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic runs the AI that helps write your report. The AI gets chart positions, no name, no email and no place.
  • Sender.net sends the email with your report.
  • Our own server, rented from a hosting company, makes the report.

How long we keep it

  • If you do not pay: your order is deleted after 24 hours.
  • Your birth data: deleted right after your report is delivered.
  • Your report PDF and a small order record (name, email, order number, dates): deleted 30 days after delivery. We keep them so we can send the report again if you lose it.
  • Payment records: kept by Gumroad under its own rules.
  • The AI service keeps the chart positions only as long as its own API rules say.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to correct it or to delete it. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Write to us at the email below.
