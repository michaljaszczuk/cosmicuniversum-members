Your details

The report is made from exactly the details you confirm before payment. A wrong birth time can change your Rising sign, houses or Moon sign. Please check your details before you pay.

Right to cancel

This is digital content made for you. When you order, you ask us to make it now. You lose your right to cancel once the report is delivered.

Refunds

We do not give refunds, except when your report never arrives. If it has not arrived within 24 hours, write to us and we fix it.
